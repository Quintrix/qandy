
   Capture the Flag
   Quintrix and Crew Software

   Capture the Flag is an attempt to create a real-time multi-player version
   of Richard Carr's classic 1992 Shareware of the Year award winning version
   'Capture the Flag'. 
   
   The game starts in the 'lobby' where there is a flag pole and 'player hats',
   click on any hat to claim it, and you can then select your avatar which
   will wear this player hat. Next tag the flag pole to be teleported to your
   team's spawn spot.
